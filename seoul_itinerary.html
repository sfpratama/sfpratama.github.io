Trending F&B Spots

Viral on TikTok · visited by Indonesian KOLs · May 2025 picks

May weather: Late May is gorgeous in Seoul — 18–24°C, sunny, minimal rain. Best time for outdoor eating and content creation at all these spots!
Michelin Bib Gourmand 2025 Must Try
Gebangsikdang (간장게장)
Mapo-gu (near Hongdae area)

The #1 viral soy-marinated raw crab in Seoul — literally called "the rice thief" because you can't stop eating rice with it. Michelin Bib Gourmand every year 2018–2025. Featured by countless Indonesian food KOLs. Set comes with seasonal banchan, crispy seaweed, and clam soup. Queue early — typically 30–45 min wait!

₩36,000–38,000 per set (~Rp 420–445K)
🚇 Subway Line 2 · Hongdae Univ. Station (5 min walk)
Viral TikTok Indonesian KOL fave
Woomoolzip (우물집)
Changsin-dong, Jongno-gu

Seoul's most viral private hot pot dining experience. Intimate traditional setting inside a beautifully preserved space — feels like stepping into a K-drama. Book via Catchtable app weeks in advance. A full evening experience perfect for couples.

₩40,000–60,000 pp (~Rp 470–700K)
🚇 Line 1 · Dongdaemun Station (10 min walk)
Indonesian KOL staple
Tosokchon (토속촌)
Jongno-gu, near Gyeongbokgung

The legendary samgyetang restaurant — whole young chicken stuffed with glutinous rice, ginseng, and dates in a rich broth. One of Seoul's most iconic restaurants, open since 1983. Featured in virtually every Indonesian KOL's Seoul trip. Warm, healing, and deeply photogenic.

₩18,000 per bowl (~Rp 210K)
🚇 Line 3 · Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 2 (5 min walk)
Viral TikTok
Sol Sot (솔솥)
Multiple Seoul locations

Famous for crispy dolsot (stone pot) rice — the ASMR sound of scorched rice scraping off the pot gets millions of TikTok views. Beautifully curated toppings, gorgeous presentation. Very popular with Southeast Asian visitors. Branch in Myeongdong is easiest to reach.

₩14,000–20,000 (~Rp 165–235K)
🚇 Line 4 · Myeongdong Station Exit 6 (3 min walk)
Every Korean KOL
Gwangjang Market (광장시장)
Jongno-gu (Seoul's oldest market)

Korea's most famous traditional market and a pilgrimage spot for every Indonesian influencer visiting Seoul. Try bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes), spicy tteokbokki, and raw beef bibimbap. Night market atmosphere is electric and super content-worthy.

₩3,000–10,000 per item (~Rp 35–120K)
🚇 Line 1 · Jongno 5-ga Station Exit 8 (1 min walk)
Viral Salt Bread
Jayeondo Salt Bread (자연도소금빵)
Multiple locations across Seoul

THE viral salt bread of Korea — buttery, flaky, slightly salty, totally addictive. Gets millions of views on TikTok with people lined up at opening time. Sold out daily before noon. Best eaten warm straight from the bag while walking. Grab it first thing in the morning!

₩2,500–4,000 each (~Rp 30–47K)
🚇 Multiple locations — nearest subway varies by branch
Indonesian KOL fave
Sancheong Charcoal Garden (산청숯불가든)
Euljiro (을지로) — hip industrial district

Authentic charcoal-grilled Korean BBQ in the hip Euljiro district — a retro industrial neighborhood that's become insanely popular with local creatives and Indonesian content creators. Smoky pork belly, old-school atmosphere, great for dinner with a group.

₩20,000–35,000 pp (~Rp 235–410K)
🚇 Line 2 · Euljiro 3-ga Station Exit 1 (3 min walk)
Viral speakeasy concept
Speakeasy Fried Chicken, Itaewon
Itaewon, Yongsan-gu

A hidden speakeasy-concept fried chicken restaurant in Itaewon that went viral on TikTok. Unique hidden-bar vibe meets Korean fried chicken perfection. The hunt to find it is part of the experience — great story to tell and amazing content to create.

₩20,000–30,000 pp (~Rp 235–350K)
🚇 Line 6 · Itaewon Station Exit 1 (5–8 min walk)

Trending & Picturesque Cafes

Aesthetic · Instagrammable · Unique concept · All TikTok-viral

Viral TikTok 2025 Top Pick
Seoul Angmusae (서울앵무새)
Seongsu-dong (+ Yongsan branch)

Neon parrot logo, rainbow-colored exterior — impossible to miss and impossible not to post. Famous for Queen Amann pastries and the unique ritual of drawing on your dessert plate with colored markers. Every visit is personal. A viral sensation since day one, now with multiple branches.

₩8,000–18,000 (~Rp 95–210K)
🚇 Line 2 · Seongsu Station Exit 3 (7 min walk)
Instagrammable May pick
Rafre Fruits (라프레)
Seochon (near Gyeongbokgung) + Seongsu

Seoul's most stunning seasonal fruit cafe — May menu features mango and the first melons of summer. Fruit displayed like art, cottage farmhouse interior, the best OJ you'll ever taste. Mango bingsu, layered fruit parfaits, fruit crepe cakes. Go at opening — sells out fast!

₩15,000–35,000 (~Rp 175–410K)
🚇 Line 3 · Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 2 (8 min walk)
TikTok viral
943 King's Cross
Hongdae, Mapo-gu

4-floor Harry Potter-themed cafe — wand boxes à la Ollivander's, grand Hogwarts-style halls, moody atmospheric lighting. Butterbeer-inspired drinks, themed pastries. Broomstick photo op outside is iconic. Even non-Potterheads will be floored by the level of detail. Global viral hit.

₩12,000–22,000 (~Rp 140–260K)
🚇 Line 2 · Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9 (5 min walk)
Full bloom in May Couple pick
Colline
Hongdae · near Sangsu Station

The most romantic cafe in Seoul — a blooming garden cafe where the seasonal floral decor changes every visit. In May, it's at peak bloom. Somehow quiet despite being in busy Hongdae. Seasonal brunch plates made with fresh ingredients. Perfect for couple content. One of Seoul's most genuinely beautiful cafes.

₩14,000–25,000 (~Rp 165–295K)
🚇 Line 6 · Sangsu Station Exit 1 (3 min walk)
Industrial chic
Daelim Changgo (대림창고)
Seongsu-dong, Seongdong-gu

The quintessential Seongsu experience — a beautifully converted warehouse with exposed brick, high ceilings, and art installations. Stunning pastries, great latte art, and the perfect backdrop for Seongsu content. A TikTok content machine that also happens to have genuinely excellent coffee.

₩7,000–14,000 (~Rp 82–165K)
🚇 Line 2 · Seongsu Station Exit 3 (6 min walk)
Best views in Seoul
Green Mile Coffee
Jongno-gu, near Bukchon Hanok Village

A sleek, modern interior cafe with an unbeatable rooftop terrace offering unobstructed panoramic views over Bukchon's traditional tiled hanok rooftops. One of the most visually dramatic cafes in Seoul. Visit during golden hour (4–6pm) for the best light. Pair with a Bukchon alley walk beforehand.

₩7,000–13,000 (~Rp 82–155K)
🚇 Line 3 · Anguk Station Exit 1 (7 min walk)
Hanok heritage
Soha Salt Pond (소하염전) — Ikseon-dong
Ikseon-dong (익선동), Jongno-gu

Set in Seoul's most photogenic hanok alley district. Traditional Korean house converted into a cozy modern cafe. Ikseon-dong itself is a must-visit — every alley is a photo op. Pair with Mil Toast and Cheese Industry nearby for a full morning of cafe hopping.

₩8,000–15,000 (~Rp 95–175K)
🚇 Line 1 · Jongno 3-ga Station Exit 4 (3 min walk)
Viral waterfall cafe
Waterfall Cafe (폭포카페)
Outskirts of Seoul (day trip)

"More waterfall than cafe" — TikTok went crazy for this. Front-row seats to a spectacular natural waterfall inside the cafe. Take a drink out and explore the surrounding trails with river and mountain views. A beautiful half-day escape from the city — best content on the whole trip.

₩7,000–12,000 (~Rp 82–140K)
🚌 Bus from central Seoul (approx 1hr, worth it!)

Concept Stores & Activewear

Retail inspo for Happyfit · Korean athleisure brands · Future-of-retail concepts

Happyfit strategy: Seoul's activewear retail scene in 2025 is arguably the most advanced in Asia. Alo Yoga's first Asia flagship + Lululemon's Korean flagship just opened. Study how these brands do experiential retail — it's the blueprint for growing a local activewear brand.
Best concept store 2025 Don't miss
Haus Nowhere (하우스 노웨어) — Gentle Monster
Seongsu-dong (13-story building)

Opened September 2025 — Gentle Monster's most ambitious project. 13 stories housing Gentle Monster eyewear, Tamburins fragrance, ATiiSSU headwear, and Nudake cafe. AI-powered installations, a giant animatronic breathing dachshund, and sci-fi art environments on every floor. Called "the future of retail" by global press. Free entry.

Free entry · Products ₩50K–₩600K
🚇 Line 2 · Seongsu Station Exit 3 (8 min walk)
Original concept store
Haus Dosan (하우스 도산)
Apgujeong, Gangnam-gu (50 Apgujeong-ro 46-gil)

The OG blueprint for experiential retail in Korea — 5-floor gallery-store. B1F: Nudake artisan dessert cafe. 2F–3F: Gentle Monster eyewear with rotating art installations. 4F: Tamburins fragrance flagship. Each floor tells a different story. Kinetic sculptures, art collabs, and an experience unlike any standard store. Open 11am–9pm.

Free entry · Nudake desserts ₩15K–30K
🚇 Line 3 · Apgujeong Station Exit 2 (12 min walk)
Alo's first Asia flagship Happyfit inspo #1
Alo Yoga Seoul — Dosan Park
Dosan Park area, Gangnam-gu

Opened July 2025 — Alo's first Asia flagship with 6 full floors: yoga studios, rooftop wellness lounge, brand cafe, immersive product experiences. Ambassadors: BLACKPINK's Jisoo, BTS's Jin. This is how the world's fastest-growing athleisure brand builds community. The single most important store visit for Happyfit's future concept.

Free entry · Apparel ₩80K–₩250K
🚇 Line 3 · Apgujeong Station Exit 2 (10 min walk)
Lululemon Korean flagship Happyfit inspo #2
Lululemon Gangnam Flagship
Gangnam district, Seoul

Korea's first Lululemon flagship — 9,000 sqft across 3 floors, opened late 2025. New retail concept centered on movement and sensory engagement. Custom 3D-printed recycled exterior. Full women's floor + dedicated menswear floor. Korea is Lululemon's fastest-growing APAC market. Study the VM, product storytelling, and community activations.

Free entry · Apparel ₩80K–₩300K
🚇 Line 2 · Gangnam Station Exit 10 (7 min walk)
"Korean Lululemon" Happyfit inspo #3
VERISH (베리쉬)
26 Yeonmujang-gil, Seongdong-gu (Seongsu)

Dubbed the "Korean Lululemon" — clean minimal aesthetics, incredibly flattering fits, pilates-to-brunch athleisure positioning. 2-story Seongsu flagship is beautifully designed. Campaign directed by Jiyeon from Single's Inferno 3 — certified cool by Korean pop culture. Super relevant direct comparison for Happyfit's positioning vs international brands.

₩40,000–120,000 (~Rp 470K–1.4Mn)
🚇 Line 2 · Seongsu Station Exit 3 (5 min walk)
Korean activewear
XEXYMIX (제이믹스)
Gangnam Station standalone store

Korea's most commercially successful activewear brand — praised for flattering, functional leggings that rival Lululemon at a fraction of the price. Worn by K-pop idols including Mamamoo's Solar. Wide color range, high-quality fabric. Excellent retail design. See how a Korean brand scales athleisure in their home market.

₩40,000–150,000 (~Rp 470K–1.75Mn)
🚇 Line 2 · Gangnam Station Exit 10 (2 min walk)
Tennis & golf athleisure
CLOVE
1F, 30 Eonju-ro 168-gil, Gangnam-gu

Beautifully curated Korean athleisure brand focused on tennis and golf lifestyle. Vibrant color palettes, incredible store design, female-forward aesthetic. Community events and active local following. A masterclass in how to build a niche activewear brand with strong community DNA — very relevant Happyfit inspo.

₩45,000–100,000 (~Rp 530K–1.2Mn)
🚇 Line 9 · Sinnonhyeon Station Exit 5 (8 min walk)
Multi-brand concept
MAB Seoul
Near Jamwon Han River Park · Sinsa Station area

Premium multi-brand concept store for runners and active lifestyle near Han River. Curated mix of premium sports brands in a clean, community-focused space. Hosts training sessions and community runs — a great model for how to build authentic brand community around sport.

Free entry · Products vary by brand
🚇 Line 3 · Sinsa Station Exit 8 (5 min walk)

Ginseng chicken · salt bread · charcoal BBQ
AM
🍜 Tosokchon — legendary samgyetang

Retro-atmosphere Korean BBQ in Euljiro. Charcoal-grilled pork belly, DIY wrapping in lettuce leaves. A classic Korean dinner experience that every Indonesian KOL visits.

🏪 CLOVE + MAB Seoul (Sinsa area)
CLOVE's gorgeous tennis/golf athleisure store, then MAB Seoul's premium multi-brand running concept store near Garosu-gil. Both within walking distance. Last major activewear research stops for Happyfit inspo.

🍜 Woomoolzip — private hot pot dinner (special!)
Reserve via Catchtable app weeks in advance. Intimate, beautiful, the kind of dinner you'll remember forever. A celebration of the trip with a perfect couple meal.

Saturday 24 May — Fruits, River & Last Night
Rafre Fruits · Sol Sot · Han River · speakeasy chicken
AM
☕ Rafre Fruits — mango & melon season!
Go at opening (typically 11am). May = mango and melon season — the most beautiful menu of the year. Mango bingsu, fruit crepe cakes, Jeju OJ. Arrive early — sells out fast on weekends.

The ASMR stone pot rice experience. Film the moment the crispy rice crust scrapes off for a guaranteed TikTok. Curated beautiful toppings. Myeongdong branch is easiest to reach.
