Myeongdong & Jongno — Central Seoul
Best base area · Day 1, 4, 6
Every Indonesian KOLNight 1
Gwangjang Market (광장시장)
📍 88 Cheonggyecheon-ro, Jongno · 9am–10:30pm daily
Korea's oldest market — bindaetteok mung bean pancakes, spicy tteokbokki, raw beef bibimbap. Goes massively viral on TikTok every month. Incredible atmosphere at night when all stalls are lit up. Every Indonesian influencer visiting Seoul comes here. Walk deep into the stalls past the entrance for the best vendors.
₩3,000–10,000 per item (~Rp 35–120K)
🚇 Line 1 · Jongno 5-ga Exit 8 · 1 min walk
Michelin Guide50+ year institution
Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자)
📍 29 Myeongdong 10-gil, Jung-gu · 10:30am–9pm
50-year-old Michelin-recognised Seoul legend for kalguksu (knife-cut noodles) and mandu dumplings. Only 4 menu items — pay before sitting. Queue moves fast. Silky noodles, deeply savoury broth, juicy dumplings. Best quick lunch near the hotel. Always packed with locals and travellers alike.
₩9,000–12,000 (~Rp 105–140K)
🚇 Line 4 · Myeongdong Exit 8 · 3 min walk
⏰ Opens 10:30am · queue before opening
ASMR Viral TikTok
Sol Sot — Jongno Branch (솔솥)
📍 46 Samil-daero 30-gil, Jongno · 11am–9pm · ⭐ 4.5
The iconic crispy dolsot stone-pot rice that gets millions of TikTok views for its ASMR scraping sound. Gorgeous toppings: bulgogi, abalone, sea bream. The moment the crust cracks off the pot is pure magic. Film it for guaranteed content. Jongno branch is easiest to reach, near Ikseon-dong.
₩18,000–28,000 (~Rp 210–330K)
🚇 Line 1 · Jongno 3-ga Exit 4 · 5 min walk

Woomoolzip (우물집)
📍 200 Changsin-gil, Jongno · 11am–3pm, 5–9pm · ⭐ 4.1
Seoul's most viral private hot pot dining — beautiful semi-private rooms on a hillside, pure K-drama atmosphere. Reserve via Catchtable app weeks in advance. Spicy tomato stew is extraordinary. Feels like stepping into a drama set. The most romantic dinner of the trip. ALWAYS fully booked — reserve immediately.
₩40,000–60,000 pp (~Rp 470–700K)

Heritage + food · Day 5
Indonesian KOL stapleMust-try
Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌)
📍 5 Jahamun-ro 5-gil, Jongno · 10am–10pm daily · ⭐ 4.2 / 11,847 reviews
The legendary ginseng chicken soup restaurant open since 1983. Whole young chicken stuffed with glutinous rice, ginseng, red dates in rich cloudy broth. Every single Indonesian KOL's Seoul list includes this. Deeply nourishing, photogenic, and deeply satisfying. Arrive before opening — queue starts forming early on weekdays.
₩18,000–22,000 (~Rp 210–260K)

Jayeondo Salt Bread (자연도소금빵)

THE salt bread of Korea — buttery, flaky, golden, slightly salty. Gets millions of TikTok views with people racing to branches at opening. Sell out DAILY before noon without exception. Best eaten warm straight from the paper bag while walking. Grab 3+ pieces. Race here first thing in the morning!

Seongsu & Euljiro (East + Hip Central)
Seoul's Brooklyn + trendy industrial · Day 2 & 6
Indonesian KOL favourite
Sancheong Charcoal Garden (산청숯불가든)
📍 Euljiro district · best for dinner
Authentic charcoal-grilled pork belly in Seoul's hip Euljiro "Hip-jiro" district. Retro industrial atmosphere, smoky charcoal grilling, communal table vibe. Every Indonesian content creator visiting Seoul includes this. After dinner, explore Euljiro's narrow alleys filled with neon-lit pojangmacha tent bars for the full experience.
₩20,000–35,000 pp (~Rp 235–410K)
🚇 Line 2 · Euljiro 3-ga Exit 1 · 3 min walk
Trending 2026
Chuan Chuan Jiu Bar (串串酒吧)
📍 Euljiro · 11:30am–10pm · closed Mondays
Seoul's #1 Sichuan mala hot pot skewer spot — bubbling spicy broth, dip-your-own skewers, sweet-sour Guo Bao Rou pork that blew everyone's mind. Drink Tsingtao to handle the numbing Sichuan buzz. Extremely viral on Korean food apps in 2025–26. Set in Euljiro's coolest industrial alley.
₩20,000–35,000 pp (~Rp 235–410K)

Hongdae & Mangwon (Western Seoul)
Youth district + local market · Day 5
Local hidden gem
Mangwon Market (망원시장)
📍 27 Poeun-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu · 9am–9pm daily · ⭐ 4.3
Seoul's most beloved local neighbourhood market — far less touristy than Gwangjang, packed with actual Koreans. Hoon Hoon Hotteok (cheese-filled sweet pancakes) is extraordinary. Jinseong Mandu has hand-sized king dumplings eaten standing on the street. Kyuseu Chicken is a must. Super authentic, very content-worthy.
₩2,000–8,000 per item (~Rp 23–95K)
🚇 Line 6 · Mangwon Station Exit 2 · 5 min walk
Chimaek culture
Oksang Dalbit — Rooftop Chimaek (옥상달빛)
📍 Hongdae · evenings from 5pm
Rooftop fried chicken + ice cold beer (chimaek) — one of Seoul's most iconic evening rituals. Oksang Dalbit is famous for their FLAMING fried chicken presentation and two styles: crispy fried and garlic pepper. Customers can doodle on the walls. Classic Hongdae rooftop experience. Book ahead on weekends.
₩25,000–35,000 pp (~Rp 290–410K)
🚇 Line 2 · Hongik Univ. Exit 9 · 8 min walk

Gebangsikdang (간장게장)
